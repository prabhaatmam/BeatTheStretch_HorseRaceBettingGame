How to Play

  1. Every race features 8 horses with randomly generated names, stats, and odds.
  2. Pick a horse and a bet amount, then place your bet.
  3. Watch the race play out — the outcome is simulated live, so favorites can lose and long shots can win.
  4. Win and you're paid your bet × odds. Lose and your bet is gone.
  5. If your bankroll hits $0, the Loan Shark will front you cash — at a price.
  6. Leave a loan unpaid twice and you'll get a warning. A third unpaid loan ends your run.
  7. Repay your loan any time you can afford it to reset your streak with the Loan Shark.
