Down the Stretch

Down the Stretch is a browser-based horse racing and betting simulation where you start with a $1,000 bankroll, choose from 8 randomly generated horses, place wagers, and watch each race play out. Every horse has different form and odds, so favorites can lose and long shots can win. The goal is to grow your bankroll, manage your bets, survive losing streaks, and avoid the Loan Shark.

How to Play:
Click Start Race.
Choose one of the 8 horses.
Review the horse's form and odds.
Choose your wager: $10, $25, $50, $100, $250, a custom amount, or Max.
Click Place Bet.
Confirm your wager with They're Off!
Watch the race or click Skip to Results.
If your horse wins, you receive bet × odds as your payout.
If your horse loses, your wager is lost.
Continue racing and manage your bankroll carefully.

Betting Rules

You start with $1,000.

Your potential payout is calculated as:

Bet Amount × Horse Odds

For example, a $50 bet at 4.0× odds pays $200 if your horse wins.

You cannot wager more than your available bankroll.


Loan Shark

If you run out of money, you can use the Loan Shark.

Borrow $500 → Owe $600

Unpaid loans create a strike streak:

1 unpaid loan: 1 strike
2 unpaid loans: 2 strikes
3 unpaid loans: Game Over

Repay your outstanding debt to reset your Loan Shark streak.


Controls

The game is designed for mouse and touch controls. No keyboard controls are required.

Click/Tap a horse — Select your horse
Bet buttons — Select a wager
Custom Bet — Enter your own wager
Max — Bet your entire bankroll
Place Bet — Continue to confirmation
They're Off! — Confirm and start the race
Skip to Results — Skip the race animation
🔊 / 🔇 — Toggle sound
Statistics — View your career stats
Loan Status — View your debt and Loan Shark status

Goal

There are no traditional levels. Your goal is to build the biggest bankroll you can while managing risk and avoiding three unpaid Loan Shark strikes.

Pick your horse. Place your bet. Beat the odds. Stay out of debt.

📱 Platform

Down the Stretch is a lightweight HTML/CSS/JavaScript browser game designed for desktop and mobile devices.

No real money is used. This is a fictional betting simulation created for entertainment purposes.
